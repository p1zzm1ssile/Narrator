Chapter 1: The Light Goes Out

The storm had been building all afternoon, and by the time the sun went down the sea was throwing itself at the rocks.

"It's gone out," said Mira, pressing her face to the window. "Grandpa, the light's gone out!"

Old Tom put down his pipe. He didn't hurry. He never hurried. "Then we'd best go and light it again," he said quietly.

"In this?" Mira whispered.

"Ships don't wait for good weather, love."

Chapter 2: The Climb

The stairs wound up and up into the dark. Somewhere above them, something banged against the glass.

"What was that?" asked Mira.

Tom laughed. "Just the wind. Nothing up here but gulls and ghosts, and the ghosts are friendly."
