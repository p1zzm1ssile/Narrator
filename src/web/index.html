Voices

Press play to hear each voice read the same few lines. Expressive voices are Chatterbox, which acts out feelings: its own voice, voices you add below, and a copy of each of your Kokoro voices that Chatterbox acts with. Characters are always given expressive voices. Fast voices are plain Kokoro, which is quick but reads flatly. The first sample of a voice takes a moment to make, then it's kept.

Kokoro isn't connected. Add its address in Settings to see its voices here.

Add a voice

Upload 10 to 20 seconds of one person speaking clearly, with no music or background noise. Chatterbox copies the voice, and it appears above as an expressive voice. Only use voices you have the right to use.
